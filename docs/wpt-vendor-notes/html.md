## Vendoring (`tests/wpt/VENDOR.md`)

Test category, added 2026-07-28 by the WPT-VENDOR backlog (`ROADMAP.md` `WPT-VENDOR-html`, `docs/wpt-status.md`), scope ⬜ (candidate). Same pinned commit, `git sparse-checkout add` at the same commit hash, **14161 files** — by far the largest vendored category (next largest: `fetch`, 985) — in 26 top-level subdirectories (`canvas`, `semantics`, `browsers`, `rendering`, `dom`, `webappapis`, `editing`, `syntax`, `interaction`, `cross-origin-opener-policy`, `cross-origin-embedder-policy`, `infrastructure`, `document-isolation-policy`, `obsolete`, `anonymous-iframe`, `user-activation`, `the-xhtml-syntax`, `links`, `capability-delegation`, `select`, `scripting`, `embedded-content`, `meta`, `iana`, `resources`, `tools`): 10263 `.html`, 1813 `.js`, 523 `.xhtml`, 298 `.png`, 260 `.yml`, 241 `.headers`, 207 `.htm`, 97 Python `wptserve` endpoints, 74 `.vtt`, 65 `.css`, 61 `.dat` (parser fixtures), 59 `.svg`. By `MANIFEST.json` it selects **8399 `testharness` ids** (701 of them flagged `testdriver`), plus 1382 `reftest`, 183 `crashtest` and 357 `manual` ids the minimal executor has no path for. **This is the first category where vendoring and surveying had to be separated**: at the measured rate of the two slices below a full `--all --root html --recursive` run is 40+ hours, which no single session can hold — the tree is pinned in one commit, and the survey proceeds subdirectory by subdirectory, with the remainder carried as explicit `WPT-VENDOR-html-*` rows in `ROADMAP.md` rather than silently dropped. Two slices surveyed so far: `html/dom` — 12 min 39 s, **192/249 harness OK, 253/4784 subtests** (the best harness-OK share in the whole backlog, 77 % vs `fetch`'s 37 %, because the subdirectory is nearly self-contained — 2 `testdriver` files and 2 `.https.` out of 478); `html/syntax` — 53 min 16 s, **69/379 harness OK, 30/675 subtests** (307 of 379 ids TIMEOUT, dominated by the documented `<iframe>`-without-browsing-context wall and by BUG-415). Seven bugs filed from the two slices: BUG-418 (the shim's DOM-arena overflow guard is dead code on the default V8 build — a page building 50 000+ nodes aborts the whole process instead of throwing `QuotaExceededError`; this alone makes all 9 `html/dom/reflection-*.html`, ~72 000 subtests, unreachable), BUG-412…BUG-416 (missing DOM APIs) and BUG-417 (a `<template>` seen before `<body>` prevents `<body>` from ever being created).
